This policy explains how **Uyir AI Labs Private Limited** ("we", "us") handles personal data on uyirailabs.com. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect
- **When you contact us:** your name, email, and anything else you choose to send (phone, company, message) and the reason you selected (project, investor, Firro demo, other).
- **When you browse:** anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (pages viewed, country, device type) through Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use advertising trackers.

## Why we use it
Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange demos or meetings you ask for, and improve the website.

## Who processes it for us
- **Web3Forms** delivers form submissions to our email inbox.
- **Zoho Mail** hosts our email.
- **Vercel** hosts the website and provides anonymous analytics.
These providers may process data outside India under their own safeguards.

## How long we keep it
Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless we enter a business relationship, then for as long as the law requires.

## Your rights
You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@uyirailabs.com. We reply within 30 days.
